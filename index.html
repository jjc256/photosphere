PhotoSphere

Stand still, spin slowly, and sweep your phone across the whole scene. PhotoSphere stitches a 360° × 180° panorama you can download.

Capture tips
  • Line the centre crosshair up with a dot, then hold still — the ring fills and the shot is grabbed automatically.
  • Pivot the phone around your body's axis — keep it close to you, don't walk.
  • Work through the dots; tap Done whenever you've covered enough.
  • Keep textured things in view (edges, objects) — blank sky or walls won't stitch.
  • Best results outdoors or in large rooms; set the FOV slider to match your lens.
